Research contribution to validate

Hypothesis: A persistent location identifier combined with time-bounded equipment assignments and integrity rules can preserve traceable maintenance history when an asset is replaced.

Measured outcomes: correct resolution, preservation of retired history, detection of missing/contradictory assignments, and behavior after IFC re-export.

Prototype computes referential-integrity checks over in-memory records; replacement preserves historical events. Integration with the IFC parser, real documents and systematic test-set evaluation remain future thesis work.
